About

I am a quantitative health researcher and statistical analyst with doctoral training in biostatistics. My work has focused on developing and applying statistical methods to complex health and genetic data, with additional experience in clinical research, health analytics, data quality, business intelligence, and research reporting.

My technical work spans traditional statistical methodology and modern data tools. I am particularly interested in opportunities involving biostatistics, epidemiology, clinical research, health services research, administrative health data, statistical programming, and health data analytics.
